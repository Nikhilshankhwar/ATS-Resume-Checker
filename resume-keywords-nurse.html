Resume Keywords for Nurses and Healthcare Staff

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Hospitals and healthcare groups increasingly use ATS software, especially for large hiring drives and jobs abroad. Your registration, certifications and unit experience are the most searched terms.

Qualifications and registration

B.Sc Nursing, GNM, Post Basic B.Sc, M.Sc Nursing, Registered Nurse (RN), State Nursing Council registration, INC, BLS, ACLS, PALS, NCLEX, OET, IELTS

Clinical skills

Patient assessment, Vital signs monitoring, Medication administration, IV cannulation, Wound care, Catheterisation, Ventilator care, Infection control, Patient education, Care planning, Triage, Emergency response, Pre- and post-operative care

Units

ICU, CCU, NICU, PICU, Emergency, Operation theatre, Medical-surgical ward, Dialysis, Oncology, Cardiac care

Systems and quality

Electronic health records (EHR), HIS, NABH standards, JCI standards, Documentation, Patient safety, Quality audits

Sample bullets

  • Cared for 4 to 6 critical patients per shift in a 20-bed ICU, including ventilator and inotrope management.
  • Maintained 100% compliance in NABH documentation audits for 2 consecutive years.
  • Trained 12 new staff nurses on infection control and medication safety protocols.

Only list a certificate you hold and that is valid on the date you apply.
