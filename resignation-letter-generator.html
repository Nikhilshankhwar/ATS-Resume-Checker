Questions

What should a resignation letter include?

Your intention to resign, your designation, your last working day, a short thank-you, an offer to help with handover, and your name. Keep it short and positive. A reason is optional.

Should I give the reason for resigning?

You do not have to. If you mention one, keep it neutral, for example a new opportunity or higher studies. Do not complain about the company or people in the letter, because it stays in your HR file.

Should I send it by email or on paper?

Most Indian companies accept email to your reporting manager with HR in copy. Some ask for a signed printed letter too. Check your HR policy.

When should I send the resignation letter?

After you have the new offer letter in writing, and after you have told your manager in person or on a call.
