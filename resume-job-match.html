Questions

What is a good resume match score?

70% or more means your resume already speaks the job’s language: apply. 50–69% means you are close: add the missing skills you really have, using the job post’s exact words, then apply. Below 50% the job may be a stretch, or your resume does not show your relevant work yet.

Should I add every missing keyword?

No. Add only skills and tools you have really used, and prove the important ones in a bullet with a result. Interviewers ask about everything on your resume.

Is this the same as an ATS score?

No. The match score compares your resume with one job. The ATS score on the main checker checks whether software can read your resume at all: format, sections, dates, contact details. You need both.
