CV Format for Gulf and Overseas Jobs (UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Europe)

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Overseas employers and agencies often screen CVs quickly for job title, years of experience, certifications and availability. Make these visible at the top.

Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait)

  • A photo is common and often expected.
  • Show total years of experience and any Gulf or international experience clearly.
  • Mention notice period and availability to travel.
  • List licences and certifications: Gulf driving licence, engineering council registration, nursing licence (DHA, HAAD, MOH, SCFHS), safety certificates (NEBOSH, IOSH).
  • Nationality is often stated; marital status only if asked.

Europe, UK, US, Canada, Australia

  • No photo, date of birth or marital status: these are usually left out.
  • 2 pages, results-focused, local spelling (CV in UK and Europe, resume in US).

Keep private

Do not put your passport number, Aadhaar or bank details on a CV. Share them only with a verified employer or licensed recruitment agency during formal processing. Be careful of agents who ask for money before an offer.

Templates: Classic with Photo for Gulf roles, any single-column template for Europe and North America.
