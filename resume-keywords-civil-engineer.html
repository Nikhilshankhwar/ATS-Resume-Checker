Resume Keywords for Civil and Site Engineers

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Civil engineering job posts in India usually ask for site execution, billing and a few software tools. Use the same words in your resume for work you have actually done.

Site execution

Site execution, Site supervision, RCC work, Shuttering, Reinforcement (BBS), Concreting, Brickwork, Plastering, Finishing works, Layout and levelling, Total station, Auto level, Quality checks, Safety (HSE), Labour management, Daily progress report (DPR)

Billing and quantity surveying

Quantity estimation, BOQ, Rate analysis, Contractor billing, RA bills, Client billing, Measurement book, Reconciliation of materials, Cost control, Tendering

Structural and design

Structural design, STAAD.Pro, ETABS, SAFE, AutoCAD, Revit, IS 456, IS 800, Working drawings, Drawing review

Project management

Project planning, Primavera P6, MS Project, Scheduling, Client coordination, Subcontractor management, Material planning, Handover

Domains people search for

Residential buildings, Commercial buildings, Highways, Bridges, Metro, Railways, Water supply, Industrial projects, Infrastructure

Sample summary

Site engineer with 4 years in high-rise residential construction. Skilled in RCC execution, BBS, quantity estimation and contractor billing, with AutoCAD and MS Project.

Sample bullets

  • Supervised RCC work for a G+14 residential tower, completing the structure 3 weeks ahead of schedule.
  • Prepared BBS and quantity estimates for 2 towers, reducing steel wastage by 6%.
  • Checked and certified monthly RA bills for 5 subcontractors worth ₹1.2 crore.
