Questions

How is in-hand salary worked out from CTC?

CTC includes things you do not get in your bank every month: the employer’s share of PF and gratuity. Take those out to get gross salary. From gross salary, your own PF (12% of basic), professional tax and income tax (TDS) are deducted. What is left, divided by 12, is your monthly in-hand salary.

What is the tax on salary under the new regime for FY 2026-27?

Budget 2026 kept the slabs unchanged: nil up to ₹4 lakh, 5% from ₹4–8 lakh, 10% from ₹8–12 lakh, 15% from ₹12–16 lakh, 20% from ₹16–20 lakh, 25% from ₹20–24 lakh and 30% above ₹24 lakh, plus 4% cess. Salaried people get a ₹75,000 standard deduction, and the rebate makes taxable income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free, so a salary up to about ₹12.75 lakh pays no tax.

What is a good salary hike when switching jobs?

In India a job switch usually brings a 20–40% hike, and more for skills in short supply. An internal yearly appraisal is usually 5–12%. Compare the in-hand amount, not only the CTC, because variable pay and benefits can make two equal CTCs very different.

Why is my in-hand lower than CTC divided by 12?

Because CTC counts employer PF, gratuity and often variable pay or insurance. Those are part of your cost to the company but are not paid to you every month.
