Expected Salary Kya Bole? Interview mein Salary ka Jawab (Freshers aur Experienced)

Updated 26 November 2026 · Resume SAT

“What is your expected salary?” sabse tricky sawal lagta hai. Bahut kam bola to nuksaan, bahut zyada bola to offer haath se ja sakta hai. Sahi tareeka hai: pehle research, phir ek range, phir flexible rehna.

Step 1: Market rate pata kariye

  • Naukri, Glassdoor, AmbitionBox par apne role, city aur experience ki salary dekhiye.
  • Job post mein salary range likhi ho to usse start kariye.
  • Apne seniors ya dosto se poochiye jo isi role mein hain.

Step 2: Apna number banaiye

  • Experienced: job change par aam taur par 20–40% hike maanga jaata hai (role aur market par depend). Apni current CTC par hike ka hisaab yahan lagaiye: Salary Hike Calculator, jismein in-hand salary bhi dikhti hai.
  • Fresher: company ka standard fresher package hota hai. Range market rate ke aas-paas rakhiye.
  • Hamesha CTC aur in-hand ka farak samajh kar boliye.

Sample answers

Fresher

“As a fresher, my priority is learning and a good start to my career. Based on what I have seen for similar roles in this city, I expect something in the range of ₹2.8 to 3.2 lakh per year. But I am open to your company’s standard package for this role.”

Experienced

“My current CTC is ₹4.5 lakh. Considering my experience and the responsibilities of this role, I am looking for around ₹5.8 to 6.2 lakh. I am flexible depending on the overall package and growth opportunities.”

Jab aap pehle unka range jaanna chahte hain

“I would like to understand the role and responsibilities better first. Could you share the budget range for this position? I am sure we can reach a figure that is fair for both of us.”

Tips

  • Exact ek number ki jagah range boliye, aur range ka neeche wala number bhi aapko theek lagna chahiye.
  • Current salary ke baare mein jhooth mat boliye. Payslip aur offer letter maange ja sakte hain.
  • Notice period bhi salary discussion mein matter karta hai: Notice Period Calculator.
  • Offer milne ke baad bhi politely negotiate kar sakte hain, khaas kar agar dusra offer ho.

Sawal-jawab

“Salary is negotiable” bolna theek hai?

Akele nahi. Ek range dijiye aur saath mein bataiye ki aap flexible hain.

Online form mein expected salary bharna zaroori ho to?

Apni research wali range ka beech ka number bhariye.
