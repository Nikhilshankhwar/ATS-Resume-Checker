Resume Keywords for Customer Support, BPO and Call Centre Jobs

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Process and channel

Inbound, Outbound, Voice process, Non-voice, Chat support, Email support, International process, Domestic process, Blended process, Back office, KYC, Collections, Technical support

Metrics

CSAT, NPS, AHT (average handle time), FCR (first call resolution), Quality score, Adherence, SLA, Productivity

Skills

Communication, English fluency, Hindi and regional languages, Active listening, Problem solving, Objection handling, Upselling, Escalation handling, Typing speed

Tools

Zendesk, Freshdesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Genesys, Avaya, Ameyo, MS Excel, CRM

Sample bullets

  • Handled 80 to 100 inbound calls a day for a telecom client with CSAT above 90%.
  • Kept AHT under 5 minutes while meeting a 95% quality score for 6 straight months.
  • Led a team of 12 chat agents; improved FCR from 72% to 81% with a shared answer library.

Freshers: mention typing speed, languages and any part-time customer-facing work.
