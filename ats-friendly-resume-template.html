An ATS-friendly template is one that applicant tracking software can read in the right order: one column, standard section names, real text (not images) and simple bullets. Most fancy templates from design websites fail at least one of these.

Get a template filled with your own resume

On Resume SAT you do not need to copy your details into a blank template. Upload your current resume (PDF, Word or even a photo), and every template previews with your own content. Pick one and download it as PDF or Word, free and without sign-up.

What makes a template ATS-safe

  • One column, read top to bottom.
  • Standard headings: Summary, Skills, Experience, Education.
  • Contact details in the body, not in the page header.
  • Common fonts and normal bullets.
  • No skill bars, icons or text inside images.

Two-column designs look good to people, but some ATS read across both columns and mix your sections. Use them when you email or print, and a single-column template when you apply on a job portal. More in our ATS resume format guide.
