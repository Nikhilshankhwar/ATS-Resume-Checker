Questions

How do I ask a stranger for a referral on LinkedIn?

Send a short connection note: who you are, the exact role and job ID, one line on why you fit, and a polite ask. After they accept, send your resume and the job link so they can refer you in two minutes.

Should I send my resume in the first message?

On LinkedIn, wait until they reply or accept. On WhatsApp or email to someone you know, attach it straight away along with the job ID.

What if they do not reply?

Send one polite follow-up after 4 or 5 days, then move on. Ask 3 to 5 people at the company instead of waiting on one.
