A biodata for a job is a short, formal document with your personal details, education, work experience and a declaration. In India it is still asked for in government and PSU posts, walk-in interviews, small companies, schools, hospitals, security and factory jobs. It is more formal than a modern resume, and usually has a photo.

Biodata vs resume vs CV

  • Biodata: personal details first, then education, experience and a declaration. Formal, often with a photo. Used for government, PSU, walk-in and local jobs.
  • Resume: 1–2 pages focused on skills and results for one job. Used by most private companies.
  • CV: a longer, complete record, used for academic, research and some overseas jobs.

Biodata format: sections in order

  1. Title and photo: “BIODATA” at the top, a recent passport-size photo on the top right.
  2. Post applied for (and advertisement number, if any).
  3. Personal details: full name, father’s name (only if asked), date of birth, gender, nationality, languages known, address, mobile, email.
  4. Educational qualifications: 10th, 12th, graduation and above, with board or university, year and marks.
  5. Work experience: organisation, designation, from–to dates and main work, latest first.
  6. Skills: computer skills, technical skills, typing speed, driving licence if relevant.
  7. Certifications and training.
  8. Hobbies (optional, 2–3 only).
  9. Declaration, place, date and signature.

BIODATA

Personal details

Languages known: Hindi, English · Mobile: 98XXXXXX10 · Email: priya.sharma@example.com
Address: Sector 12, Dwarka, New Delhi – 110075

Educational qualifications

Work experience

Skills

Declaration
I hereby declare that the information given above is true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief.
Place: New Delhi · Date: ________ · Signature: ________

Biodata for freshers

  • Put education right after personal details, with marks.
  • Add internships, training, college projects and certificates in place of experience.
  • Write computer skills clearly: MS Office, Tally, typing speed, programming languages.
  • Keep it to one page.

Photo and personal details: what to include

  • Use a recent, clear, passport-size photo with a plain background, only if the job asks for one.
  • Add personal details such as father’s name, marital status or religion only if the employer asks. Most private jobs do not need them.
  • Never write Aadhaar, PAN, bank or passport numbers on a biodata. Share them only in an official form when asked.

Common mistakes

  • Spelling mistakes in name, email or phone number.
  • Marks and years that don’t match your certificates.
  • A blurry selfie as the photo.
  • Forgetting the signature and date when submitting a printed copy.
  • Fancy colours and borders: keep it plain and readable.

Biodata kaise banaye? (Hindi tips)

Job ke liye biodata mein sabse upar “BIODATA” likhiye aur right side photo lagaiye. Phir in sections ko isi order mein likhiye: personal details (naam, janm tithi, mobile, email, pata), education (10th, 12th, graduation, saal aur marks), experience (company, post, kab se kab tak), skills (computer, typing), aur aakhir mein declaration ke saath place, date aur signature. Aadhaar ya PAN number biodata par mat likhiye. Aasaan tareeka: Resume SAT par apni details daaliye aur Govt / Biodata template chuniye.

Questions

Is biodata the same as resume?

No. A biodata focuses on personal details and is more formal; a resume focuses on skills and results. Many government and local jobs still ask for a biodata.

How many pages should a biodata be?

One page for freshers, up to two pages with experience.

Should I write the declaration on a biodata?

Yes. Government and many traditional employers expect a declaration with place, date and signature at the end.
