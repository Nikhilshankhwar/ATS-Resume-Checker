How to Make a Resume for an Internship (With Example)

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

An internship resume is one page and shows that you are ready to learn and can do basic work in the field. Projects, coursework and college activities count as evidence.

Sections

  1. Name and contact (phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub or portfolio)
  2. Objective or summary (2 lines)
  3. Education (current degree, year, CGPA if 7+)
  4. Skills
  5. Projects (2 to 4)
  6. Certifications and online courses
  7. Activities and achievements

Sample summary

Third-year B.Tech Electrical student with projects in PLC automation and solar design, seeking a summer internship in power or automation.

Projects that impress

  • Say what you built, with which tools, and what it achieved.
  • Link to code, drawings or a report if you can.
  • Group projects are fine: say what your part was.

Where to apply

Internshala, LinkedIn, Naukri Campus, company career pages, PSU summer training programmes and your college placement cell.
