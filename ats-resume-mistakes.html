12 Resume Mistakes That Get You Rejected by ATS

Updated 18 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Most rejections are not about your ability. They happen because the software could not read the resume, or because the resume did not use the words the recruiter searched for. Here are the mistakes we see most often, and how to fix them.

  1. Two-column or designed templates. The parser may read across both columns and mix your skills into your job history. Use one column.
  2. Text inside images or icons. Logos, skill bars, icons for phone and email, and infographics are invisible to most ATS. Write everything as plain text.
  3. Scanned or photo PDFs. If you cannot select the text, the ATS reads nothing. Export from Word or Google Docs instead.
  4. Contact details only in the header. Some parsers skip headers and footers. Put your name, phone and email in the body.
  5. Creative section names. Use “Experience”, “Skills” and “Education” so the system knows what each part is.
  6. Missing keywords from the job post. If the job says “Power BI” and you wrote “Microsoft dashboards”, a search for Power BI will miss you.
  7. Skills listed but never shown. Back up key skills in your bullets. Some systems weigh skills that appear in experience more.
  8. Inconsistent or unclear dates. Use one format such as “Aug 2024 – Present” so the ATS can calculate your experience.
  9. Duty lists instead of results. “Responsible for testing” is weak. “Tested 40 panels per week with zero rework” is strong. See our action verb list.
  10. Very long bullets and paragraphs. Keep bullets to one or two lines so both software and people can scan them.
  11. Spelling and grammar errors. A misspelled skill (“Javascipt”) will not match a search, and errors hurt your credibility.
  12. Unneeded personal details. Date of birth, father's name, religion, marital status and a declaration use space without helping you.
