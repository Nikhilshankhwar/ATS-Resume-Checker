What to write in a 12th pass resume

  • Objective: the job you want and 2–3 skills (typing, computer, communication).
  • Education: 12th and 10th with board, year and marks.
  • Experience: part-time work, family shop, tuition, delivery or any job you have done. All of it counts.
  • Skills: typing speed, MS Word, Excel basics, billing, driving licence, languages.
  • Certifications: CCC, DCA, Tally, spoken English or any short course.

Tips

  • Use a simple email made from your name, not a nickname.
  • Write your typing speed if you know it (for example 30 WPM).
  • Bring 2–3 printed copies to every walk-in interview.
  • Learn a short computer course like CCC or DCA. It adds a strong line to your resume.

Questions

Which jobs can I get after 12th?

Data entry, back office, BPO/customer support, retail sales, delivery partner, telecaller, office assistant and apprentice roles.

Do I need a resume for a 12th pass job?

Yes. Even for walk-ins, a one-page resume makes a good first impression.
