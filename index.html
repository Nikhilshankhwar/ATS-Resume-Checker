Method

How the score works

The score has two halves. Both must be good for a resume to reach a recruiter and then convince one.

Can the software read it?

ATS software turns your file into plain text and sorts it into fields. Columns, tables, icons, image-only PDFs and unusual section names break that step. These checks look at the file itself and at how cleanly its text comes out.

Is the content strong?

Once parsed, the text is ranked against the job and then read by a person. These checks measure numbers in your bullets, action verbs, weak phrasing, spelling, length, and how many of the job's skills you cover.

Questions

FAQ

What is an ATS, and why should I care?

An applicant tracking system is the software most large companies use to collect applications. It converts your resume to text, stores it in fields and lets recruiters search and rank candidates. If it cannot read your file, or your resume lacks the job's keywords, a recruiter may never open it.

What is a good score?

80 or above means the resume parses cleanly and reads well. 60 to 79 is a conditional pass with clear gaps to fix. No checker can give a certified ATS score, because every ATS product behaves differently, so use the number as a direction and the failed checks as your to-do list.

Can an ATS read PDFs, columns, icons and charts?

Text PDFs and Word files are read well. Scanned or image-only PDFs are not read at all. Two-column layouts and tables are often read across the page, which mixes lines together. Icons, emoji, charts and skill bars are dropped or turned into stray characters, so write the information as plain text.

Is my resume uploaded or stored anywhere?

Scoring, PDF reading, fixes, the editor and downloads all run inside your browser. Nothing is saved after you close or refresh the page. The AI buttons are the one exception: when you click one, your resume text is sent through this site's server to Groq, an AI service, to be rewritten. Groq's terms do not allow it to train AI models on that text.

Will the AI upgrade invent experience I do not have?

It is instructed to use only the facts in your resume and to leave a placeholder such as [X%] where a number is missing. Always read the preview before you use it, and replace every placeholder with a real figure.

Why does my score change when I add a job description?

Without a job description, the score covers parsing, sections, content and red flags. Adding one unlocks four more checks (hard skills, soft skills, job title and keywords) and a job match percentage. If your resume lacks most of the skills the job asks for, the overall score is capped, because a well-written resume for the wrong job will still be filtered out. Paste the full job posting, not just the title, for an accurate match.

Which template should I pick?

For job portals, pick one marked ATS-safe: Corporate Classic for MNC and banking roles, Tech MNC for IT, Core Engineering for power, EPC and manufacturing, Campus Fresher for graduates. Career Timeline and Monogram add a premium look and stay single-column. The two sidebar templates use two columns, which some ATS software mixes up, so keep those for emailing a recruiter directly or for print.

Does it cost anything?

No. Everything is free and needs no account. The AI buttons use a free AI service with a daily limit, so on a busy day they may pause until the next day while everything else keeps working.
