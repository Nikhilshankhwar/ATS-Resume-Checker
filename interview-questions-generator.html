Questions

How do I answer "Tell me about yourself"?

In about one minute: your current role and years of experience, two specific things you have done with a number or result, and why this job is the next step. Do not repeat your whole resume.

How should I answer behavioural questions?

Use the STAR method: the Situation, the Task you had, the Action you took, and the Result, ideally with a number. Prepare 4 or 5 real stories that you can adapt to different questions.

How many questions should I prepare?

Prepare the 10 to 15 most likely questions properly, and practise saying the answers out loud. Also prepare 2 or 3 questions to ask the interviewer at the end.
