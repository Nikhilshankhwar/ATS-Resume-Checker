Resume Keywords for Sales and Marketing Jobs

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Sales and marketing recruiters look for two things: the right words for the role, and numbers that show results. A sales resume without targets or revenue numbers looks weak, even with all the right keywords.

Sales and business development

B2B sales, B2C sales, Business development, Lead generation, Cold calling, Client acquisition, Key account management, Channel sales, Dealer network, Distributor management, Sales target achievement, Pipeline management, Negotiation, Closing, Upselling, Cross-selling, Territory management

Tools

Salesforce, Zoho CRM, HubSpot, LeadSquared, MS Excel, Google Sheets, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Power BI

Digital marketing

SEO, SEM, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Social media marketing, Content marketing, Email marketing, Performance marketing, Google Analytics, A/B testing, Conversion rate optimisation, Campaign management, Influencer marketing

Marketing and brand

Brand management, Market research, Go-to-market strategy, Product marketing, Trade marketing, Event marketing, Marketing budget, Competitor analysis

Numbers that work well

  • % of target achieved (for example, 118% of annual target)
  • Revenue or order value closed
  • Number of new accounts, dealers or clients
  • Leads, cost per lead, click-through rate, ROAS for marketing

Sample bullets

  • Achieved 124% of the 2025 sales target and closed 35 new B2B accounts in Delhi NCR.
  • Built a network of 18 dealers in 2 new districts, growing regional revenue by ₹60 lakh.
  • Cut cost per lead by 30% by moving budget from search to Meta lead ads after A/B tests.
