Skills to Put on a Resume: 200+ Hard and Soft Skills by Job

The skills section is the part an ATS searches most. List the skills the job asks for that you really have, and prove the important ones in your experience or project bullets.

Hard skills vs soft skills

Hard skills are tools and methods you can be tested on: Excel, SQL, AutoCAD, GST filing, PLC programming. Soft skills are how you work: communication, leadership, problem solving. ATS filters mostly search hard skills.

How many to list

8 to 15 is a good range. Group them if you have more, for example “Software: …”, “Tools: …”, “Languages: …”.

Hard skills by field

  • Electrical and SCADA: SCADA, RTU, IEC 60870-5-104, Modbus, PLC, Relay testing, AutoCAD Electrical. Full list
  • Mechanical: CNC, TPM, Lean, FMEA, SolidWorks, GD&T. Full list
  • Civil: Site execution, BBS, BOQ, STAAD.Pro, Primavera. Full list
  • Software: Java, Python, React, SQL, AWS, Docker, Git. Full list
  • Data: SQL, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Python, Statistics. Full list
  • Accounts: Tally, GST, TDS, Reconciliation, SAP FICO. Full list
  • Sales and marketing: Lead generation, CRM, Negotiation, SEO, Google Ads. Full list
  • HR: Recruitment, Payroll, PF/ESIC, HRMS. Full list
  • Nursing: Patient care, IV cannulation, ICU, BLS, ACLS. Full list

Soft skills recruiters search for

Communication, Teamwork, Leadership, Problem solving, Time management, Client handling, Adaptability, Attention to detail, Decision making, Stakeholder management, Mentoring, Negotiation

Prove your skills

Writing “Leadership” in a list proves nothing. A bullet does: “Led a team of 6 technicians to commission 4 substations on schedule.” Our checker shows which listed skills never appear in your bullets.

Skills not to list

  • MS Word and internet browsing (assumed for office jobs)
  • Skills you used once in college and cannot discuss
  • Star ratings or percentage bars: ATS cannot read them
