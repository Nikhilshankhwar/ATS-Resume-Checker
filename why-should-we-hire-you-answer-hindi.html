Why Should We Hire You? Best Answer Kaise De (Freshers aur Experienced, Hindi)

Updated 26 November 2026 · Resume SAT

“Why should we hire you?” ka matlab hai: “Aap baaki candidates se better kyun hain, aur hamari problem kaise solve karenge?” Yeh apne aap ko job ki zaroorat se match karke dikhane ka mauka hai.

Formula: Job ki zaroorat + aapka proof + attitude

  1. Job description se 2–3 main zarooratein uthaiye (jaise Tally, GST, client handling). JD Keyword Finder se nikaal sakte hain.
  2. Har zaroorat ke saath apna proof dijiye: internship, project, ya pichli job ka result, number ke saath.
  3. Ek line attitude ki: jaldi seekhna, zimmedari, lambe samay tak tikna.

Sample answer: fresher

“This role needs someone who knows Tally, GST basics and Excel, and who can work accurately. In my internship I entered over 500 vouchers in Tally and helped prepare GST data for 15 clients, so I can start contributing quickly. I have also built Excel reports with pivot tables. As a fresher I bring energy and the willingness to learn, and I am looking for a long-term role where I can grow with the company.”

Sample answer: experienced

“You need someone who can grow sales in a new territory and manage dealers well. In the last four years I have done exactly this: I grew my territory by 28% in one year and added 60 new retailers. I also know this region and its distributors well, so I will not need much time to settle in. I am target-driven and I keep my collections on time, which I understand is important for this role.”

Sample answer: career change

“Although my background is in operations, I have spent the last year building data skills. I completed SQL and Power BI courses and built three dashboards that my team uses every week. My operations experience helps me understand what the numbers mean for the business, which many fresh analysts don’t have. I am serious about this switch and ready to put in the effort.”

Kya na bole

  • “Because I need a job.”
  • “I am hard-working and honest” bina kisi proof ke.
  • Doosre candidates ki burai.
  • Bahut lamba answer: 45–60 seconds kaafi hai.

Sawal-jawab

Fresher ke paas experience nahi hai, to proof kya de?

Internship, college project, certification, part-time kaam, ya college mein li gayi koi zimmedari.

Isi tarah ka sawal “What makes you a good fit?” ho to?

Same formula use kariye.
