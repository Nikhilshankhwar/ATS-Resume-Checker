Workday Resume Tips: Format, Autofill and Application Checklist (2026)

Many large companies (Indian MNCs, banks, consulting and product firms) take applications on Workday. You can tell from the web address: it usually contains myworkdayjobs.com. Workday asks you to create an account, then offers “Autofill with Resume”, which reads your file and fills the form. When your resume is not laid out the way it expects, details land in the wrong boxes or stay empty.

How Workday reads your resume

  • It reads top to bottom in one stream. A side column with skills or contact details gets mixed into your work history.
  • It looks for standard headings to decide where each line goes: Work Experience, Education, Skills.
  • It fills each job as separate fields: Job Title, Company, Location, From, To and Role Description.
  • It does not fill the Skills and Languages boxes from your resume. You type those yourself.

Workday resume format checklist

  1. One column, no tables, text boxes, icons or photos of text.
  2. Name, email, phone and city at the top, in the body, not in the header area.
  3. One line per job: Job Title, Company — Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY (for example: Accounts Executive, Kapoor Traders — Apr 2021 – Present).
  4. Month and year for every start and end date. “3 months” or “2022” cannot fill a From/To box.
  5. Plain headings: Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications. Don’t combine two in one heading.
  6. Text PDF or Word (.docx). Both work if the layout is simple.
  7. A LinkedIn URL in the contact line.

After you click “Autofill with Resume”

  • Open every job entry and check the title, company and dates. Fix anything that landed in the wrong field.
  • Paste each role description from your resume if it is empty or cut short.
  • Type your skills one by one in the Skills box and pick the closest match from the list.
  • Answer the screening questions honestly: they filter before a recruiter sees you.

Questions

PDF or Word for Workday?

Either. A one-column text PDF or a clean .docx both read well. What breaks parsing is the layout, not the file type.

Why does Workday ask me to retype everything?

Usually because of columns, tables, or dates written in an unusual way. Fix those and autofill gets much better.

Do I need a new account for every company?

Usually yes. Each company runs its own Workday site, so the account is per company. Use the same email each time.
