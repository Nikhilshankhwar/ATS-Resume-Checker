Naukri Profile Kaise Banaye? Recruiter Calls Badhane ke 10 Tips (Hindi mein)

Updated 26 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Naukri.com par recruiter khud candidates search karte hain. Aapki profile kitni baar dikhegi, yeh is baat par depend karta hai ki aapki profile kitni complete hai, kitni recent update hui hai, aur usmein sahi keywords hain ya nahi. Yeh guide batata hai ki Naukri profile kaise banaye aur kaise improve kare.

Step 1: Basic details sahi bhariye

  • Poora naam, professional photo, sahi mobile aur email (verify kariye).
  • Current location aur preferred locations: recruiter city se filter karte hain.
  • Total experience, current salary, notice period sach-sach likhiye. Notice period kam ho to calls zyada aati hain. Dekhiye: Notice Period Calculator.

Step 2: Resume headline

Recruiter search mein aapke naam ke saath yahi line dikhti hai. Format: Job title | Experience | 2–3 skills. Jaise: “Accounts Executive | 3 Yrs | Tally, GST, TDS”. Dekhiye Resume headline kaise likhe ya Naukri Profile Headline Generator.

Step 3: Key skills (sabse zaroori)

  • Recruiter Naukri par keywords se search karte hain. Jo skill aapki profile mein nahi, us search mein aap nahi dikhenge.
  • Apne role ke 10–15 skills daaliye, wahi naam jo job posts mein likhe hote hain (jaise “Tally Prime”, “GST”, “Power BI”).
  • Apne role ki list yahan dekhiye: Naukri Key Skills Finder.

Step 4: Profile summary

3–5 lines mein: aap kya karte hain, kitne saal se, main skills, aur ek achievement number ke saath. Free mein banaiye: Naukri Profile Summary Generator. Formula ke liye dekhiye Resume summary kaise likhe.

Step 5: Employment, education, projects

  • Har job mein designation, dates aur 2–3 lines mein kaam aur results.
  • Freshers: projects aur internship zaroor bhariye.
  • Certifications aur IT skills section bhi bhariye. Har complete section se profile strength badhti hai.

Step 6: ATS-friendly resume upload kariye

Naukri par uploaded resume bhi software padhta hai. Upload se pehle Resume SAT par ATS score check kariye aur file ka naam rakhiye: Naam_Role_Resume.pdf.

Recruiter calls badhane ke 10 tips

  1. Profile har 2–3 din mein update kariye (koi chhoti line bhi badal dijiye). Recently active profiles upar dikhti hain.
  2. Subah 9–11 baje ke beech update kariye, jab recruiters search karte hain.
  3. Key skills mein job posts wale exact words likhiye.
  4. Headline mein job title aur experience zaroor likhiye.
  5. Profile 100% complete kariye.
  6. Photo professional rakhiye.
  7. Preferred location mein 3–4 shahar daaliye agar shift ho sakte hain.
  8. Recruiter messages ka jaldi jawab dijiye.
  9. Roz 5–10 relevant jobs par apply kariye; track rakhiye: Job Application Tracker.
  10. Har 3 mahine mein resume naya upload kariye.

Sawal-jawab

Naukri profile free hai?

Haan, profile banana aur apply karna free hai. Paid services zaroori nahi hain.

Naukri par calls kyun nahi aa rahi?

Aksar wajah: key skills kam hain, headline mein job title nahi, profile purani hai, ya notice period lamba hai. Upar ke 10 tips follow kariye.

Naukri profile aur resume alag ho sakte hain?

Dono mein same jobs, dates aur skills hone chahiye. Mismatch dikhe to recruiter ko doubt hota hai.
