About Resume SAT

A free ATS resume checker, built in India.

Most large companies use an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) to read resumes before a recruiter sees them. A good candidate can be rejected simply because the ATS could not read a two-column layout, missed a skill written in a different way, or found no match with the job description.

Resume SAT shows you how your resume looks to that software and helps you fix it, for free and without signing up.

What it does

  • Scores your resume on 29 checks in five groups: ATS essentials, sections, content, job tailoring and red flags.
  • Compares your resume with a job description and shows the skills and keywords you are missing.
  • Finds grammar mistakes, weak verbs, long bullets and places where numbers would make your work stronger.
  • Lets you edit your resume and download it as PDF or Word in 13 ATS-safe templates.
  • Offers optional AI help to rewrite bullets and find skills you already used but did not list.

Who made it

Resume SAT was created by Nikhil Shankhwar, an electrical engineer from New Delhi working on SCADA and power systems. After seeing friends and colleagues struggle to get past resume filters, he built a tool that explains exactly what an ATS sees and works for every field, from engineering and IT to accounts, sales, healthcare and hospitality.

How the score works

The score is a careful estimate based on how common ATS products read resumes. Real ATS software differs from company to company, so use the score as a guide, not a guarantee. Full details are on the How scoring works section.

Free to use

There is no subscription and no sign-up. The site may show ads to cover running costs.
