How to Write a Fresher Resume With No Experience

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Every recruiter hiring freshers knows you have no full-time experience. They are looking for proof that you can learn and do the work. Your projects, internships, skills and results in college are that proof.

1. Use the right order

  1. Name and contact (phone, email, city, LinkedIn, GitHub if relevant)
  2. Summary (2 lines)
  3. Skills
  4. Education
  5. Projects
  6. Internships and training
  7. Certifications
  8. Achievements and activities

2. Write a short, specific summary

B.Tech Electrical Engineering graduate (2026) with a 6-week internship in substation maintenance. Skilled in AutoCAD Electrical and PLC basics, and looking for a graduate engineer trainee role.

3. Treat projects like jobs

Give each project a title, tools used and 2 or 3 bullets with what you did and the result.

  • Made a project on solar energy.
  • Designed a 1 kW rooftop solar system for a hostel block and calculated a 4.5-year payback period.

4. Count everything you have done

  • Internships, apprenticeships and industrial training (even 2 to 4 weeks)
  • Freelance or part-time work, tuition, family business
  • College clubs, fests and events you organised
  • Online certifications from NPTEL, Coursera, Google or AWS

5. Match each job post

Read the job description and use its words for skills you really have. See how to tailor your resume.

6. Keep it to one page

Remove school-level details beyond Class 10 and 12 marks, hobbies with no link to the job, and personal details like father's name and date of birth.

Common fresher mistakes

  • An objective like “to work in a reputed company”: replace with a real summary.
  • Listing 25 skills you only heard of.
  • Fancy two-column templates from Canva that ATS cannot read. See ATS-friendly format.
