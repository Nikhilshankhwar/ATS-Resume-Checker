Fresher resume format (sections isi order mein)

  1. Naam aur contact: naam, mobile, professional email, city, LinkedIn (agar hai). Poora ghar ka pata, photo, DOB zaroori nahi hai.
  2. Resume headline: ek line, jaise “B.Com Fresher | Tally, GST, Excel”. Dekhiye: Resume headline kaise likhe.
  3. Career objective ya summary: 2–3 lines. Dekhiye: Resume summary kaise likhe.
  4. Education: degree, college, saal, marks. Fresher ke liye education upar rahegi.
  5. Skills: technical skills (Excel, Tally, Java, AutoCAD) aur 2–3 soft skills.
  6. Projects: college project, final-year project, personal project: kya banaya, kaunse tools, kya result.
  7. Internship / training: company, kitne mahine, kya kaam kiya.
  8. Certifications: NPTEL, Coursera, Google, Tally certificate wagairah.
  9. Achievements / activities: college fest, NSS, NCC, sports, competitions (sirf 2–3).

Experience nahi hai to kya likhe?

  • Projects ko experience ki tarah likhiye: “Built a library management app in Java and MySQL; 200+ students used it.”
  • Internship chhoti ho to bhi likhiye: 1 mahine ki internship bhi kaam aati hai.
  • Part-time kaam: tuition padhana, family business, freelancing, event volunteering: sab likh sakte hain.
  • Har line action verb se shuru kariye: Built, Designed, Managed, Prepared, Analysed. Dekhiye: Action verbs.

ATS-friendly banane ke 6 rules

  • Single column, simple font (Arial, Calibri), koi table ya photo mein text nahi.
  • Headings simple rakhiye: Education, Skills, Projects, Experience.
  • Job description ke keywords skills aur projects mein daaliye. Dekhiye: JD Keyword Finder.
  • File PDF ya Word mein bhejiye, naam rakhiye: Priya_Sharma_Resume.pdf.
  • Ek page kaafi hai.
  • Bhejne se pehle ATS score check kariye.

Common galtiyan

  • Funny ya purana email (cooldude123@…).
  • Har job ke liye ek hi resume.
  • “Hobbies: listening music, watching movies”: yeh jagah projects ko dijiye.
  • Spelling mistakes: ek baar zor se padh kar check kariye.
  • Colourful Canva template jismein ATS text padh hi na paaye.
