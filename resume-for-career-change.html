How to Write a Resume When Changing Careers

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

When you change fields, your old job titles do not match the new jobs, so an ATS may not find you. Lead with what connects you to the new role.

1. Headline and summary for the new role

Electrical engineer moving into data analytics. 3 years of plant data reporting in Excel, now skilled in SQL, Python and Power BI with 3 portfolio projects.

2. A skills section built from the new job descriptions

Use the keywords of the target role that you have truly learned or used.

3. Projects section above experience

Show 2 to 4 projects in the new field with tools and results. Link to GitHub, a portfolio or a report.

4. Rewrite old experience for transferable skills

  • Responsible for substation maintenance.
  • Tracked maintenance data for 40 feeders in Excel and built monthly fault reports used by plant management.

5. Add learning

Certifications, courses and bootcamps relevant to the new field.

Common switches: core engineering to IT or data, teaching to training or HR, sales to marketing, BPO to HR or operations.
