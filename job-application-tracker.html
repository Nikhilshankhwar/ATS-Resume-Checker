Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. The tracker keeps everything in your browser’s local storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data removes it, so export a copy now and then.

How many jobs should I apply to each week?

Quality beats volume. 10 to 20 well-matched applications a week, each with a resume adjusted to the job description, usually gets more interviews than 100 one-click applications.

When should I follow up?

About 7 to 10 days after applying, and 2 to 5 working days after an interview if you have not heard back. Set the follow-up date here and the row turns orange when it is due. Use the follow-up email writer for the message.
