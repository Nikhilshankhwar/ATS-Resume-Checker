Questions

What should a fresher put on a resume?

Your education, technical skills, 1–3 projects with what you did and the result, any internship or training, certifications and achievements. A short summary at the top says which job you want. One page is enough.

Should a fresher write an objective or a summary?

A 2–3 line summary is better than a generic objective. Say your degree, the role you want and your strongest skills or project.

Should I add Class 10 and 12 marks?

Many Indian recruiters still look at them for freshers, especially for campus and PSU roles. Add them if they are good; leave them out after a few years of experience.
