100+ Strong Action Verbs for Your Resume (By Category)

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

The first word of each bullet sets the tone. “Responsible for maintenance” describes a duty. “Reduced breakdowns by 20% through preventive maintenance” shows a result. Start every bullet with a strong, past-tense action verb (present tense only for your current job, if you prefer).

Weak phrases to replace

Instead ofTry
Responsible forManaged, Led, Owned, Ran
Worked onBuilt, Developed, Delivered, Implemented
Helped / Assisted withSupported, Contributed to, Enabled
Was part of a team thatCollaborated with, Partnered with
Did / HandledExecuted, Processed, Resolved, Coordinated
Made betterImproved, Optimised, Streamlined, Upgraded

Leadership and management

Led, Managed, Directed, Supervised, Mentored, Coached, Trained, Delegated, Coordinated, Headed, Oversaw, Spearheaded, Mobilised, Hired, Onboarded

Building and engineering

Designed, Developed, Built, Engineered, Commissioned, Installed, Configured, Programmed, Integrated, Implemented, Deployed, Tested, Calibrated, Automated, Upgraded, Migrated, Prototyped

Problem solving and maintenance

Resolved, Troubleshot, Diagnosed, Fixed, Restored, Repaired, Debugged, Identified, Investigated, Prevented, Rectified

Improvement and results

Improved, Increased, Reduced, Cut, Saved, Accelerated, Boosted, Optimised, Streamlined, Simplified, Standardised, Eliminated, Doubled, Exceeded, Achieved

Analysis and planning

Analysed, Assessed, Evaluated, Forecast, Modelled, Measured, Audited, Reviewed, Researched, Mapped, Planned, Prioritised, Estimated, Validated

Communication and documentation

Presented, Documented, Reported, Drafted, Authored, Briefed, Negotiated, Persuaded, Explained, Published, Translated

Sales, business and customers

Generated, Secured, Won, Closed, Expanded, Launched, Acquired, Retained, Grew, Pitched, Upsold, Partnered

Finance and operations

Reconciled, Budgeted, Allocated, Processed, Filed, Controlled, Procured, Scheduled, Tracked, Complied, Verified

Turn a verb into a strong bullet

Use the pattern: Verb + what you did + how (tool or method) + result (number).

  • Responsible for RTU testing at sites.
  • Tested and commissioned RTUs at 12 substations using IEC 60870-5-104, cutting site handover time by 2 days.
  • Worked on monthly reports.
  • Automated monthly sales reports in Excel and Power BI, saving 6 hours of manual work each month.

Only use numbers that are true. If you do not have an exact figure, describe the scale instead (number of sites, users, machines or clients).
