MBA Fresher Resume: Format, Summary and Examples

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

MBA placement resumes are short and dense. Recruiters look for the summer internship, live projects, academic rank and leadership roles.

Order

  1. Name, contact, LinkedIn
  2. Education (MBA, graduation, Class 12, Class 10 with scores)
  3. Summer internship
  4. Work experience (if any)
  5. Live projects and competitions
  6. Positions of responsibility
  7. Certifications and skills

Summer internship bullets

  • Marketing: Analysed 1,200 customer reviews and proposed 3 product changes; one was adopted for the next launch.
  • Finance: Built a DCF valuation model for 4 listed auto companies; findings used in the team's sector report.
  • HR: Designed an onboarding survey for 300 new joiners and cut first-month attrition questions by 40%.
  • Operations: Mapped a warehouse picking process and suggested a layout change that cut picking time by 12%.

Tips

  • One page only.
  • Every bullet should have a number or a clear outcome.
  • Put case competition wins and club leadership roles; they matter in campus placements.
