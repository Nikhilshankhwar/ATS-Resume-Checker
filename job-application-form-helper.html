Why autofill gets your resume wrong

  • Two columns or tables. These systems read straight down the page, so a side panel gets mixed into your work history.
  • Contact details in a side panel or at the bottom. The form looks for your email and phone near your name.
  • Missing or partial dates. “3 months” or “2022” cannot fill a From and To month-year box.
  • Creative or combined headings. “Education and Certifications” or “My Journey” makes the parser guess where lines belong.
  • Skills. Workday’s “Autofill with Resume” does not fill the Skills and Languages boxes at all, so you type them yourself.

Questions

Does this fill the company form for me automatically?

No. For your safety it never logs in to any site. It gives you each field ready to copy, so you can paste it into the form in seconds and check it as you go.

Is my resume uploaded anywhere?

No. The splitting and the checks run inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Which file should I upload to Workday?

A one-column text PDF or a Word (.docx) file both work. Avoid two-column designs, tables and photos of text.

Why does Taleo show my details in the wrong boxes?

Older Taleo sites read Word files more reliably than PDF, and expect plain headings and MM/YYYY dates. Download the Word version from the kit and upload that.

Does the same resume work on Naukri and LinkedIn?

Yes. An ATS-safe resume that works on Workday and Taleo also reads well on Naukri, LinkedIn and Indeed.
