Resume Keywords for Electrical, SCADA and Automation Engineers

Updated 18 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Electrical and automation job descriptions are full of specific standards, protocols and tools. Recruiters search for those exact words. Use the ones you have really worked with, in the same spelling the job uses.

Core electrical keywords

Power distribution, Substation (33/11 kV, 66 kV, 132 kV, 220 kV), Switchgear, Transformers, Circuit breakers, Protection relays, Relay coordination, Earthing, Cable sizing, Load flow, Single line diagram (SLD), Panel wiring, Testing and commissioning, Preventive maintenance, Electrical safety, LOTO, IS/IEC standards

SCADA, RTU and communication

SCADA, RTU, IED, HMI, IEC 60870-5-101, IEC 60870-5-103, IEC 60870-5-104, IEC 61850, Modbus RTU / TCP, DNP3, OPC UA, GPS time synchronisation (NTP / PTP), Data acquisition, Alarm and event handling, Point mapping, IO list, FAT / SAT, Communication networks, Fibre optics, Ethernet switches, Network troubleshooting

PLC and automation

PLC programming, Ladder logic, Function block, Siemens TIA Portal, Allen-Bradley Studio 5000, Schneider, ABB, Delta, DCS, VFD, Instrumentation, Sensors, Control panels, P&ID, Loop checking, Industrial automation

Railway and metro traction

Traction power, OHE, Receiving substation (RSS), Auxiliary substation (ASS), Traction substation (TSS), Power SCADA, Metro rail, Indian Railways, RDSO, Interlocking interface

Software and tools

AutoCAD Electrical, EPLAN, ETAP, MATLAB, MS Excel, SCADA software (for example WinCC, iFIX, Zenon), Network tools such as Wireshark

Soft skills that job posts ask for

Site coordination, Client handling, Documentation, Team leadership, Vendor management, Safety compliance, Problem solving

Sample summary

SCADA and RTU engineer with 2+ years in traction power distribution projects for metro and railway clients. Hands-on with IEC 60870-5-103/104, Modbus and GPS time synchronisation, from IO list preparation to FAT, SAT and commissioning.

Sample bullets

  • Prepared and verified IO lists for 66 kV and 25 kV receiving substations, mapping 1,200+ points to the SCADA control centre.
  • Commissioned RTUs and IEDs over IEC 60870-5-104 at 8 stations, completing SAT with zero open punch points.
  • Resolved protocol and network faults between RTUs and the control centre, restoring data for critical feeders within the same shift.
