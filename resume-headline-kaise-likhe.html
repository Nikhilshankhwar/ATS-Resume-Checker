Resume headline woh ek line hai jo aapke naam ke neeche hoti hai aur batati hai ki aap kaun hain aur kya kaam karte hain. Naukri par jab recruiter search karta hai, to aapke naam ke saath sabse pehle yahi line dikhti hai. Isliye yahi decide karti hai ki recruiter aapki profile kholega ya nahi.

Resume headline me kya likhe? (Formula)

[Job title ya degree] | [Kitne saal ka experience] | [2–3 main skills] | [koi proof, agar sach hai]

  • Job title wahi likhiye jo job posts mein likha hota hai, jaise “Data Analyst”, “Accounts Executive”.
  • Skills wahi likhiye jo aapko sach mein aati hain: Excel, Tally, SQL, AutoCAD.
  • Har part ko “|” se alag kijiye. Poora sentence mat likhiye.
  • Naukri par limit 250 characters hai, lekin 80–180 characters kaafi hain.

Galat vs sahi headline

  • ✕ “Hard-working aur dedicated insaan” → ✓ “Accounts Executive | GST, Tally, Excel | 3 Saal”
  • ✕ “Fresher, job chahiye” → ✓ “BCA Fresher | Python, SQL | Data Analyst Roles”
  • ✕ “Engineer” → ✓ “Site Engineer Civil | 4 Saal | RCC, BBS, Billing”

Naukri par headline kaise badle?

Naukri app ya website kholiye → View profile → Resume headline ke paas pencil icon → nayi headline likhiye → Save. Headline badalne se profile “recently updated” bhi dikhti hai.

Aksar puchhe jaane wale sawaal

Resume headline aur summary mein kya fark hai?

Headline ek line hoti hai. Summary 2–4 line ki hoti hai aur headline ke neeche aati hai.

Kya headline English mein likhni chahiye?

Haan. Recruiters English keywords se search karte hain, isliye headline English mein hi likhiye.

Fresher kya likhe?

Degree aur saal, jo role chahiye, aur ek proof: internship, project ya certificate.
