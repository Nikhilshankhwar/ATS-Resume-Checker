Resume Headline Examples for Freshers and Experienced (Naukri and LinkedIn)

A resume headline is the one line under your name that says who you are. On Naukri it is a separate field, and recruiters often search it. A clear headline with the job title and two key skills works best.

Formula

[Job title] | [Years or level] | [2 to 3 key skills or domain]

Freshers

  • B.Tech Electrical Graduate | Substation and PLC Internship | AutoCAD Electrical
  • Computer Science Fresher | Java, Spring Boot, React | 2 Deployed Projects
  • B.Com Graduate | Tally Prime, GST Basics, Advanced Excel
  • MBA Marketing Fresher | Digital Campaigns, Google Ads, Market Research
  • Mechanical Engineering Graduate | CNC, AutoCAD, SolidWorks | GET Aspirant
  • Civil Engineering Fresher | Site Execution Internship | AutoCAD, BBS
  • BCA Graduate | Python, SQL, Power BI | Aspiring Data Analyst
  • B.Sc Nursing Graduate | ICU Internship | BLS Certified

Engineering

  • SCADA and RTU Engineer | 2+ Years Metro and Railway Projects | IEC 104, Modbus
  • Electrical Maintenance Engineer | 5 Years | HT/LT Systems, Preventive Maintenance
  • Production Engineer | 4 Years Auto Components | Lean, TPM, Kaizen
  • Site Engineer | High-Rise Residential | RCC Execution, Billing, QS
  • Quality Engineer | IATF 16949 | PPAP, FMEA, 8D
  • Design Engineer | SolidWorks, GD&T | Sheet Metal Products

IT and data

  • Java Backend Developer | 4 Years | Spring Boot, Microservices, AWS
  • Full Stack Developer | React, Node.js, MongoDB | 3 Years
  • Data Analyst | SQL, Power BI, Python | Retail and E-commerce
  • QA Engineer | Selenium, API Testing, Agile | 3 Years
  • DevOps Engineer | Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD | AWS Certified
  • Network Engineer | CCNA | Routing, Switching, Firewalls

Business, finance and others

  • Accounts Executive | GST, TDS, Month-End Closing | Tally and SAP
  • B2B Sales Manager | Industrial Products | 120% of Target in 2025
  • HR Recruiter | IT Hiring | End-to-End Recruitment, Naukri, LinkedIn
  • Digital Marketing Executive | SEO, Meta Ads, Content | 3 Years
  • Customer Support Lead | Voice and Chat | CSAT 92%+
  • Staff Nurse | 5 Years ICU | BLS, ACLS

Tips

  • Use the job title the jobs you want actually use.
  • Put numbers only if they are true.
  • Avoid “Hard-working and dedicated professional”, which says nothing.
