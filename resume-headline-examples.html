A resume headline is the one line under your name that tells a recruiter who you are and what you do. On Naukri it is a separate field called Resume Headline, and it shows next to your name when recruiters search, so it decides whether they click on your profile. A clear headline with your job title and 2 or 3 key skills works best.

The formula

[Job title or degree] | [Years or level] | [2–3 key skills, tools or domain] | [one proof point, if true]

Use the pipe “|” to separate parts: it reads faster than commas. Skip full sentences like “I am a hard-working professional”.

Naukri profile headline: rules that matter

  • Limit: the Naukri Resume Headline field takes up to 250 characters. Aim for 80–180 so the important words show in search results.
  • Where to edit: Naukri → View profile → Resume headline → pencil icon.
  • Use the job title recruiters search for, exactly as job posts write it (“Data Analyst”, not “Data Enthusiast”).
  • Put your strongest keyword first. Recruiters read the first 6–8 words.
  • Update it when you change focus. A fresh edit also marks your profile as recently updated.

Weak vs strong headlines

  • ✕ “Hard-working professional seeking a challenging role” → ✓ “Data Analyst | SQL, Power BI | 3 Years Retail”
  • ✕ “Fresher looking for job” → ✓ “B.Com Fresher | Tally Prime, GST, Excel | Accounts Trainee”
  • ✕ “Engineer at XYZ” → ✓ “Electrical Maintenance Engineer | 5 Years | HT/LT, Preventive Maintenance”

Resume headline vs LinkedIn headline

Use the same core: role plus key skills. LinkedIn allows a longer headline (up to 220 characters) and you can add your field or a proof point. Our LinkedIn profile checker scores your headline and suggests better ones.

Resume headline me kya likhe? (Hindi tips)

Resume headline mein apna job title, experience aur 2–3 main skills likhiye, “|” se alag karke. Jaise: “Accounts Executive | 3 Saal | GST, Tally, Excel”. “Hard-working” jaise shabd mat likhiye. Questions

What is the character limit for the Naukri resume headline?

250 characters. Keep the important words in the first 80–100 characters.

Should a fresher write a resume headline?

Yes. Use your degree, the role you want and one proof point (internship, project or certification).

Is a resume headline the same as a summary?

No. The headline is one line; the summary is 2–4 lines below it with more detail.
