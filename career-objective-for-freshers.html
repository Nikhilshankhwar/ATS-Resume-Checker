Career Objective for Freshers: 25 Examples That Are Not Generic

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Most fresher resumes start with “To work in a reputed organisation where I can utilise my skills”. Recruiters skip it because every resume says it. A good objective is 2 lines, names the role you want and the skills you bring.

Formula

[Degree] graduate with [skill or internship] seeking a [role] where I can [specific contribution].

Engineering

  • Electrical engineering graduate with hands-on substation internship experience, seeking a graduate engineer trainee role in power distribution or SCADA projects.
  • Mechanical engineering graduate skilled in AutoCAD and SolidWorks, looking for a production or design trainee role in manufacturing.
  • Civil engineering graduate with site execution training on an RCC building, seeking a junior site engineer role.
  • Electronics graduate with PLC and embedded C projects, seeking an automation or testing engineer trainee role.

IT and data

  • Computer science graduate with two deployed full-stack projects in React and Node.js, seeking a junior software developer role.
  • BCA graduate skilled in Python, SQL and Power BI, looking for a data analyst trainee role.
  • B.Tech IT graduate with CCNA training, seeking a network support engineer role.
  • Fresher with Selenium and manual testing certification, seeking a QA trainee role.

Commerce, MBA and others

  • B.Com graduate trained in Tally Prime and GST returns, seeking an accounts assistant role.
  • MBA (Finance) graduate with an equity research internship, seeking a financial analyst role.
  • MBA (HR) graduate with campus hiring internship experience, seeking an HR executive role.
  • MBA (Marketing) graduate with a live digital campaign project, seeking a digital marketing executive role.
  • B.Sc Nursing graduate with ICU and emergency internship, seeking a staff nurse position.
  • Hotel management graduate with front-office training at a 5-star hotel, seeking a guest relations role.

Objective or summary?

An objective says what you want. A summary says what you offer. Recruiters prefer a summary, even for freshers. See resume summary examples.
