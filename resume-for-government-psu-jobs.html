Resume Format for Government Job: PSU, Railway, Bank and SSC (2026)

Most government exams (SSC, UPSC, banking exams, railway recruitment) use an online application form, not a resume. But a resume or biodata is still asked for in many cases: PSU lateral and contractual hiring, project posts in government departments and institutes, walk-in interviews, deputation and consultant roles. These resumes follow a more formal style than private-sector resumes.

Government vs private-sector resume

  • Order: government formats usually put personal details and educational qualifications first; private resumes lead with a summary and experience.
  • Education in a table: exam, board or university, year, percentage or CGPA, division.
  • Exact dates for every job (DD/MM/YYYY), with designation, organisation, pay level or CTC if asked.
  • Photo, declaration and signature are often required; private-sector resumes usually skip them.
  • Plain language: no design, colours or icons. One column, standard fonts.

Need the simpler biodata used for walk-ins and local jobs? See Biodata format for job.

Sample layout (follow the advertisement first)

  1. Name, post applied for and advertisement number
  2. Contact: mobile, email, address for correspondence
  3. Personal details only as asked: date of birth (for age limit), category if applicable, nationality
  4. Educational qualifications (table): 10th, 12th, graduation, post-graduation with year and marks
  5. Exam scores if relevant: GATE score, year and rank for PSU posts; NET/JRF for research posts
  6. Work experience: from–to dates, designation, organisation, key responsibilities (3–5 points each)
  7. Projects and their results, written plainly
  8. Certifications and trainings
  9. Languages known
  10. Declaration, place, date and signature

Sample declaration

I hereby declare that the information given above is true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief. Place: ______ Date: ______ (Signature)

Mistakes that get applications rejected

  • Dates or marks that don't match your certificates.
  • Missing the post name or advertisement number.
  • Not following the format or document list given in the notice.
  • Experience counted wrongly against the cut-off date in the advertisement.
  • Sharing Aadhaar, PAN or passport numbers on the resume when the form doesn't ask for them.

Railway, bank and PSU notes

  • PSU (through GATE): the online form is the application; a resume helps in the interview round. Highlight your GATE score, final-year project and internships.
  • Railway and metro contractual posts: show exact experience in the domain asked (signalling, traction, civil, IT) with project names and dates.
  • Banks (specialist officers, contractual IT and risk roles): list certifications (CAIIB, CISA, CCNA and so on) and the exact years of experience the notice asks for.

Questions

Is a biodata the same as a resume?

In government hiring, “biodata” usually means a formal resume with personal details, education in a table, a declaration and a signature.

Should I write an objective?

A one-line objective naming the post is fine for government formats, for example “Seeking the post of Assistant Engineer (Electrical) under Advt. No. 12/2026.”

How many pages?

1–2 pages is normal. Attach certificates only if the advertisement asks.
