Resume Format for Government and PSU Jobs

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Most government recruitment uses online application forms, not resumes. A resume or “biodata” is still asked for in PSU lateral hiring, contractual and project posts, interviews and walk-ins. The style is more formal than private-sector resumes.

What to include

  • Full name, contact details and address for correspondence.
  • Educational qualifications in a table: degree, institute, board or university, year, percentage or CGPA.
  • Exam scores if relevant, such as GATE score and year for PSU posts.
  • Experience with exact dates, designation, organisation and pay level if asked.
  • Key projects and responsibilities, written plainly.
  • Certifications and training.
  • Personal details only as the advertisement asks (for example date of birth for age limits, category if applicable).
  • A declaration and signature if the advertisement requires it.

Photo

Many government formats ask for a recent photo. Our Classic with Photo template adds one neatly; add a photo only if the notice asks.

Tips

  • Follow the advertisement's format exactly, including order and document list.
  • Keep dates and marks consistent with your certificates.
  • Never share identity numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, passport) on a resume unless the official form asks for them.
