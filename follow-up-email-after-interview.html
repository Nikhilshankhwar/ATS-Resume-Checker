Questions

When should I send a thank-you email after an interview?

The same day, or the next morning at the latest. Keep it to 4 or 5 lines and mention one thing from the conversation.

How long should I wait before following up?

If the recruiter gave a date, wait until one or two working days after it. If not, wait about a week after the interview, or 7 to 10 days after applying. Follow up once or twice, not more.

How do I decline a job offer without burning bridges?

Reply quickly, thank them, give a short honest reason without details, and say you would like to stay in touch.
