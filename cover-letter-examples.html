Cover Letter Examples for Freshers and Experienced (India)

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Many applications in India do not ask for a cover letter, but when they do (or when you email a recruiter), a short, specific one helps. Keep it under 200 words.

Structure

  1. Opening: the role you are applying for and where you saw it.
  2. Why you fit: 2 or 3 points that match the job, with one result.
  3. Why this company: one honest line.
  4. Close: availability and thanks.

Example: fresher engineer

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Graduate Engineer Trainee (Electrical) role posted on your careers page. I completed my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering in 2026 and did a 6-week internship in substation maintenance, where I helped test protection relays and prepared single line diagrams in AutoCAD.

Your work on metro power systems is the field I want to build my career in. I can join immediately and would welcome the chance to discuss how I can contribute.

Regards,
[Your name], [phone]

Example: experienced professional

Dear [Name],

I am writing about the Data Analyst opening. In my 3 years at a retail company I built Power BI dashboards used by 200 sales staff and automated weekly reports with Python, saving about 5 hours a week.

Your job description mentions SQL, Power BI and stakeholder reporting, which is exactly my daily work. My notice period is 30 days. I have attached my resume and would be glad to talk.

Best regards,
[Your name]

Tips

  • Use the hiring manager's name if you can find it.
  • Do not repeat your whole resume.
  • Mention notice period if you are working.
  • Send as PDF or in the email body, as the job post asks.
