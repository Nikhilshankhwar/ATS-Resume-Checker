LinkedIn Profile Tips to Get Found by Recruiters

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Check your own resume in 30 seconds. Upload a PDF or Word file and see how an ATS reads it. Free, no sign-up. Check my resume →

Recruiters search LinkedIn much like an ATS: by job title, skills and location. A complete profile that matches your resume shows up more.

Checklist

  • Photo: a clear, recent face photo with a plain background.
  • Headline: job title and 2 or 3 key skills, not just “Looking for opportunities”. See headline examples.
  • Location: your city, and preferred cities in Open to Work.
  • About: 4 to 6 lines: what you do, key skills, one or two results, and what you are looking for.
  • Experience: same titles and dates as your resume, with 2 or 3 bullets each.
  • Skills: add up to the maximum, starting with the most important for your target jobs.
  • Open to Work: set job titles and locations; you can choose to show it only to recruiters.
  • Custom URL: linkedin.com/in/yourname, and put it on your resume.

Keep it active

Post or comment on topics in your field once a week. Ask 2 or 3 colleagues for recommendations.
