Tell Me About Yourself Kaise Bole? Best Answer Freshers aur Experienced ke liye (Hindi)

Updated 26 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Lagbhag har interview is sawal se shuru hota hai: “Tell me about yourself.” Interviewer aapki life story nahi sunna chahta. Woh dekhna chahta hai ki aap kaun hain, kya kar sakte hain, aur yeh job aapke liye sahi kyun hai, woh bhi 1–2 minute mein, confidence ke saath.

3-step formula: Present → Past → Future

  1. Present: abhi aap kya hain ya kya karte hain. (“I am a B.Com graduate…” / “I am working as…”)
  2. Past: 1–2 cheezein jo aapne seekhi ya achieve ki: internship, project, result, number ke saath.
  3. Future: aap yeh job kyun chahte hain aur company ke liye kya karenge.

Total 60–90 seconds. Naam, father ka naam, ghar ka address, hobbies ki lambi list: inki zaroorat nahi, yeh sab resume mein hai.

Sample answer: fresher (B.Com)

“Thank you for the opportunity. I am Priya, a B.Com graduate from Delhi University. During my 3-month internship at a CA firm, I entered over 500 vouchers in Tally and helped with GST return data and bank reconciliation for 15 clients. That is where I found that I enjoy accounts work because it needs accuracy and discipline. I have also completed a Tally Prime with GST course. Now I am looking for an Accounts Executive role where I can handle day-to-day bookkeeping and grow into GST and finance work. That is why this role at your company excites me.”

Sample answer: fresher (B.Tech / IT)

“I am Arjun, a Computer Science graduate from AKTU. I enjoy building practical software. In my internship at CodeNest, I built four REST APIs in Spring Boot and fixed more than 20 bugs. In college, I made a library management app that over 200 students used. I am strongest in Java, SQL and React, and I practise problem-solving regularly. I want to start my career as a software engineer in a team like yours, where I can work on real products and learn good engineering practices.”

Sample answer: experienced (Sales, 4 years)

“I have four years of experience in field sales in FMCG distribution, currently with ABC Foods in Lucknow. I manage 120 retailers and last year I grew my territory sales by 28% by adding 60 new outlets. Before that, I worked as a sales trainee where I learnt dealer handling and collections. Now I am looking for a bigger territory and a team-lead path, and your company’s growth in my region makes this role a strong next step for me.”

Sample answer: experienced (Customer Support, 2 years)

“I have two years of experience as a Customer Support Executive in a voice and chat process. I handle around 80 queries a day and my CSAT is 92%. I was also chosen to train new joiners on our CRM. I enjoy solving customer problems quickly and calmly. I am now looking for a role with more responsibility, like escalations or quality, and I believe I can add value to your support team from day one.”

Galtiyan jo nahi karni

  • Resume ko line-by-line padh dena.
  • Family details, “my father is…, my mother is…” se shuru karna.
  • 3–4 minute tak bolte rehna. 90 seconds ke andar rakhiye.
  • Ratta maarke robot ki tarah bolna. Points yaad rakhiye, words nahi.
  • Pichli company ki burai karna.

Taiyari kaise kare

  • Apna answer likhiye, phir phone par record karke suniye.
  • Har job ke liye “Future” wali line badaliye: company ka naam aur role.
  • Answer mein wahi baatein rakhiye jo resume mein hain, taaki interviewer aage wahi pooche jo aapko achhe se aata hai.

Sawal-jawab

Kya Hindi mein answer de sakte hain?

Agar interview Hindi mein ho raha hai to haan. Private companies mein zyada-tar English expect ki jaati hai, isliye simple English mein taiyari kariye.

Hobbies batani chahiye?

Sirf ek, aur sirf tab jab woh job se juda ho ya kuch khaas ho (jaise state-level sports).

Aur sawal?

Dekhiye: Why should we hire you, Strengths and weaknesses, Expected salary kya bole.
