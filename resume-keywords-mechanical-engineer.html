Resume Keywords for Mechanical and Production Engineers

Updated 18 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Mechanical job posts are split into a few clear tracks: production, maintenance, quality, design and projects. Recruiters search for the tools and methods of their track, so use the exact names of the ones you have used.

Production and manufacturing

Production planning, Shop floor management, CNC machining, VMC, Lathe, Assembly line, Cycle time reduction, Line balancing, Capacity planning, Kaizen, 5S, Lean manufacturing, OEE, Shift handling, Manpower handling

Maintenance

Preventive maintenance, Breakdown maintenance, Predictive maintenance, TPM, MTBF, MTTR, Root cause analysis, Hydraulics, Pneumatics, Compressors, Pumps, Utilities, Spare parts planning, Maintenance logs

Quality

Quality control, Quality assurance, ISO 9001, IATF 16949, PPAP, APQP, FMEA, Control plan, SPC, MSA, 8D, Poka-yoke, First article inspection, Vernier, Micrometer, CMM, Six Sigma

Design

AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, Creo, NX, ANSYS, GD&T, 2D drafting, 3D modelling, BOM, Sheet metal design, Tolerance stack-up, DFM, Product development

Projects, HVAC and utilities

HVAC, MEP, Piping, Plumbing, Fire fighting systems, Chillers, AHU, Installation and commissioning, Site execution, BOQ, Vendor coordination

Sample summary

Mechanical engineer with 3 years in CNC production and preventive maintenance at an auto-components plant. Skilled in TPM, root cause analysis and SolidWorks, with a record of cutting downtime and rejection.

Sample bullets

  • Reduced machine downtime by 18% across 3 CNC lines by introducing a weekly preventive maintenance plan.
  • Cut rejection rate from 4.5% to 2.8% with in-process checks and an 8D review of repeat defects.
  • Led 5S and Kaizen activities in the machining section, freeing 120 sq ft of floor space.
