Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Resume SAT is a free online tool that checks how well a resume will be read by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your resume stays in your browser

When you upload a PDF, Word file or paste text, the resume is read and scored inside your own browser. The file is not uploaded to our servers and is not stored. Nothing is saved between visits: when you refresh or close the page, your resume is gone.

AI features

Some optional buttons (for example “Upgrade entire resume”, “Rewrite weak bullets” and “Find more skills with AI”) use artificial intelligence. Only when you click one of these buttons, the text of your resume (and the job description, if you added one) is sent through our server to Groq, an AI provider, to produce the rewritten text. We do not store this text. Groq processes it under its own privacy policy. If you do not want your resume to be processed by AI, simply do not use those buttons; every other feature works without them.

Reviews and feedback

If you submit a review, we store the rating and text you write, your name and email if you choose to give them (both optional), and basic technical details such as browser type, screen size, file type and the score shown, so that we can fix problems. Your resume is never sent with a review. Your email is used only to reply to you and is never sold or shared.

Cookies and advertising

Resume SAT itself does not set tracking cookies. The site may show advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Other services we use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may record standard server logs such as IP address.
  • Cloudflare runs our small server for AI requests and reviews, and may process IP addresses to prevent abuse.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr deliver fonts and the PDF, Word and OCR (image reading) libraries. OCR runs inside your browser, so a scanned resume is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits so we know how many people use the site. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.

Security

The checker page carries a security policy that tells your browser to block it from sending data anywhere except the services listed above. If any outside library were ever tampered with, it still could not send your resume to an unknown address.

Children

Resume SAT is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can use the resume checker without giving any personal information. To ask for a review you sent to be deleted, contact us through the Contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
