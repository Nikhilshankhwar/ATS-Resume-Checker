Questions

How is the last working day calculated?

Most companies count the notice from the day you resign. With a 90-day notice and the resignation date counted as day 1, the last working day is 89 days after the resignation date. Some companies count from the next day. Check the notice clause in your offer letter or HR policy.

Can I use my leave to shorten the notice period?

Many companies allow earned leave to be adjusted against the notice period, but it is the company’s choice. Ask HR in writing. Enter those days above to see the earlier date.

What is a notice period buyout?

You pay the company, or your new employer pays it, for the days you do not serve. It is usually worked out on basic salary (some companies use gross) for the days bought out. Your full and final settlement will show the exact amount.

Do weekends and holidays count in the notice period?

Yes. The notice period counts calendar days, not working days, unless your contract says otherwise.
