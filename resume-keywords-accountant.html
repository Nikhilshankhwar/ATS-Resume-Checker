Resume Keywords for Accountants and Finance Professionals

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Finance and accounts job descriptions in India repeat a clear set of terms. If you have done the work, use these exact words so recruiters can find you.

Accounting

General ledger, Journal entries, Accounts payable (AP), Accounts receivable (AR), Bank reconciliation, Vendor reconciliation, Month-end closing, Year-end closing, Trial balance, Fixed assets, Accruals, Petty cash, Invoicing

Tax and compliance (India)

GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9), Input tax credit, TDS returns, Form 26AS, Income tax, E-way bill, E-invoicing, ROC filings, Statutory audit, Internal audit, Ind AS

Financial analysis and reporting

Financial statements, P&L, Balance sheet, Cash flow, MIS reports, Budgeting, Forecasting, Variance analysis, Cost accounting, Working capital, Ratio analysis

Software

Tally Prime, Tally ERP 9, SAP FICO, Oracle, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Busy, MS Excel (VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, Pivot tables), Power BI

Qualifications people search for

CA, CA Inter, CMA, ACCA, CPA, M.Com, B.Com, MBA Finance

Sample summary

Accounts executive with 3 years in GST, TDS and month-end closing for a manufacturing company. Skilled in Tally Prime and SAP FICO, with monthly MIS reporting to management.

Sample bullets

  • Filed monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B for 3 GST registrations with no late fees or notices.
  • Reconciled 40+ vendor accounts each month, recovering ₹4.5 lakh in duplicate payments.
  • Built an Excel MIS dashboard that cut month-end reporting from 5 days to 2.
