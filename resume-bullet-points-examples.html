Resume Bullet Points: 50 Examples That Show Results

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

Bullet points are where recruiters decide if you can do the job. Strong bullets start with an action verb, say what you did and end with a result, ideally a number.

Formula

Action verb + what you did + how (tool or method) + result

Engineering

  • Commissioned 12 RTUs over IEC 60870-5-104 across metro substations, finishing SAT 1 week early.
  • Reduced breakdowns by 22% by introducing a monthly preventive maintenance plan for 40 machines.
  • Cut material wastage by 6% by preparing accurate BBS for a G+14 tower.
  • Resolved 60+ field faults in SCADA and PLC networks, keeping availability above 99%.

IT and data

  • Built REST APIs in Spring Boot serving 50k requests a day with 99.9% uptime.
  • Reduced page load time from 4.2 s to 1.6 s by code-splitting and caching.
  • Automated a weekly Excel report with Python, saving 5 hours a week.
  • Built a Power BI dashboard used by 200 sales staff for daily targets.

Sales, marketing and customer roles

  • Achieved 124% of annual target and opened 35 new B2B accounts.
  • Grew Instagram followers from 2k to 15k in 6 months with a weekly content plan.
  • Resolved 70 customer queries a day while keeping CSAT above 92%.

Finance and HR

  • Filed monthly GST returns for 3 registrations with zero late fees.
  • Reconciled 40 vendor accounts monthly and recovered ₹4.5 lakh in duplicates.
  • Hired 45 engineers in 4 months, cutting time to hire from 38 to 24 days.

Freshers

  • Designed a 1 kW rooftop solar system for a hostel and calculated a 4.5-year payback.
  • Built a college event app in Flutter used by 800 students during the fest.
  • Organised a technical fest for 1,200 participants with a team of 15 volunteers.

No numbers?

Use scale instead: how many sites, machines, users, clients or team members. Never invent figures. See our action verbs list.
