Resume summary 2–4 lines ka chhota paragraph hai jo resume ke sabse upar, aapke naam aur contact ke neeche hota hai. Recruiter resume par pehle 6–8 second deta hai, aur sabse pehle yahi padhta hai. Achhi summary batati hai ki aap kaun hain, kya kar sakte hain, aur company ko kya fayda denge.

Resume summary me kya likhe? (Formula)

[Job title] with [kitne saal ka experience] in [field/industry]. Skilled in [2–3 main skills]. [Ek result ya achievement, number ke saath]. Looking to [company ke liye kya karenge].

  • Job title wahi likhiye jis job ke liye apply kar rahe hain.
  • Skills job description se uthaiye, taaki ATS keywords match karein.
  • Number zaroor daaliye: kitne clients, kitna target, kitne percent improvement.
  • “I”, “me”, “my” mat likhiye. Seedha shuru kijiye.

Summary vs objective: kya farak hai?

  • Summary: aapne kya kiya aur kya kar sakte hain. Experience walon ke liye best.
  • Objective: aap kya seekhna ya karna chahte hain. Freshers isko use kar sakte hain, lekin skills aur projects ke saath. Dekhiye: Career objective for freshers.

Common galtiyan

  • “Hard-working, honest, dedicated” jaise khaali words. Inki jagah proof likhiye.
  • Bahut lambi summary: 4 lines se zyada mat likhiye.
  • Har job ke liye ek hi summary bhejna. Job title aur skills har baar job ke hisaab se badaliye.
  • Jhooth ya badha-chadha kar likhna: interview mein pakda jaata hai.

Sawal-jawab
