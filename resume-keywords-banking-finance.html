Resume Keywords for Banking and Finance Jobs

Sales and relationship roles

Relationship management, Customer acquisition, Cross-selling, CASA, Retail banking, Wealth products, Mutual funds, Insurance, Home loans, Personal loans, Business loans, Portfolio management, Target achievement

Credit and risk

Credit appraisal, Credit underwriting, Financial statement analysis, CIBIL, Due diligence, Risk assessment, Collections, NPA management, KYC, AML

Operations

Branch operations, Cash management, Clearing, NEFT and RTGS, Account opening, Compliance, Audit, RBI guidelines

Analyst roles

Financial modelling, Valuation, Equity research, Excel, Power BI, Variance analysis, Budgeting

Certifications

JAIIB, CAIIB, NISM, IRDA, CFA, FRM

Sample bullets

  • Opened 180 new CASA accounts and sourced ₹2.4 crore in home loans in 2025, 115% of target.
  • Appraised 60+ MSME loan files a month with a first-time approval rate of 85%.
  • Reduced 90+ day overdue accounts by 18% in the branch through weekly follow-ups.
