Resume Summary Examples for Freshers and Experienced

Updated 19 November 2026 · Resume SAT

The summary sits at the top of your resume and is often the first thing a recruiter reads. Keep it to 2–3 lines. It should say who you are, what you are good at, and what you want, using words from the job description.

The formula

[Job title or degree] with [years] of experience in [domain]. Skilled in [2–4 key skills or tools]. [One result or strength] and looking for [target role].

Avoid empty lines like “Hard-working, dynamic individual seeking a challenging position”. They tell the reader nothing.

Freshers

  • B.Tech Electrical: Electrical engineering graduate (2026) with internship experience in substation maintenance and PLC programming. Comfortable with AutoCAD Electrical and MATLAB, and eager to grow in power distribution projects.
  • Computer science: B.Tech CSE graduate skilled in Java, Spring Boot and React, with two deployed full-stack projects on AWS. Looking for a software developer role.
  • B.Com: B.Com graduate trained in Tally Prime, GST basics and advanced Excel, with a 3-month internship in a CA firm. Seeking an accounts assistant role.
  • MBA marketing: MBA (Marketing) with a live project in digital campaigns that grew Instagram followers by 40%. Skilled in Google Ads, Meta Ads and Excel.

Experienced

  • SCADA engineer: SCADA and RTU engineer with 2+ years on metro and railway traction power projects. Hands-on with IEC 60870-5-104, Modbus and commissioning from FAT to SAT.
  • Software engineer: Backend engineer with 4 years building Java and Spring Boot microservices for fintech. Led a migration to Kubernetes that cut hosting costs by 30%.
  • Data analyst: Data analyst with 3 years in SQL, Python and Power BI. Built dashboards used by 200+ sales staff for weekly targets.
  • Accountant: Accountant with 5 years in GST, TDS, audits and month-end closing. Expert in SAP FICO and Tally, with zero compliance penalties in the last 3 years.
  • Sales: B2B sales executive with 4 years selling industrial equipment. Achieved 120% of annual target in 2025 and opened 18 new dealer accounts.
  • Mechanical: Mechanical engineer with 6 years in production and maintenance of CNC lines. Reduced machine downtime by 25% through a TPM programme.
  • Nurse: Registered nurse with 5 years in ICU care, BLS and ACLS certified. Experienced with ventilator management and electronic health records.
  • Customer support: Customer support lead with 3 years in voice and chat support for an e-commerce brand, keeping CSAT above 92% for a 12-person team.

Tips

  • Change the summary for each job: copy the job title and 2–3 skills from the posting.
  • Write in third person without “I” (“Electrical engineer with…”, not “I am an electrical engineer…”).
  • Put the numbers in your experience bullets; one strong figure in the summary is enough.
