Questions

How do I download my LinkedIn profile as a PDF?

Open your own profile on LinkedIn in a browser. Click Resources (on some accounts More) under your name and choose Save to PDF. The PDF downloads straight away.

What makes a strong LinkedIn headline?

Your target role, then 2 or 3 skills or tools recruiters search for, and your field or a proof point, for example “Data Analyst | SQL, Power BI, Excel | Retail and E-commerce”. Avoid just “Engineer at Company”, because it hides your skills from search.

How long should the About section be?

Aim for 1,000 to 2,000 characters: who you are and what you work on, 2 or 3 results with numbers, your main skills, and what you are looking for. The first 2 lines show before “see more”, so make them count.

How many skills should I add on LinkedIn?

LinkedIn allows up to 100 skills. Add the 20 to 50 that match the jobs you want, with the exact names used in job posts, and pin the 3 most important ones.

How do I get a custom LinkedIn URL?

On your profile, click Edit public profile and URL on the right, then edit the URL to your name, for example linkedin.com/in/nikhil-shankhwar. It looks cleaner on your resume.
